SpoolNest privacy policy

Publisher: YouthTeamHQ Limited, trading as YTHQ Studios
Package: com.ythqstudios.spoolnest
Support and privacy contact: support@ythqstudios.com
Effective: 23 November 2026 (version 2.0)

Your records stay on your device

The app stores spool names, materials, brands, colours, storage notes, quantities, prices, currency codes, print logs with their usage and cost, drying logs, printer names and power use, empty-spool weights and app settings in private device storage. It does not require an account or send these records to a YTHQ Studios server. There is no separate analytics SDK, cloud sync, microphone, camera, contacts or precise-location feature.

Automatic Android cloud backup and device transfer are disabled. Export a backup before changing devices or uninstalling. Deleting an item removes it from the app; clearing app storage or uninstalling removes all local records and preferences. These actions do not delete backups you exported elsewhere.

Backup files and sharing

You choose each backup file and its destination through Android's document picker. A backup contains the app's saved records, their local identifiers and the app's settings (currency, electricity price, low-stock level, default printer and sort order). It does not contain purchase information. The chosen storage provider may have its own privacy policy. Restoring a compatible backup replaces the app's saved records only after your confirmation. The app does not request broad file-storage access.

Where a Share action is available, you choose which app or person receives the reorder list or spool label using Android's share sheet. We do not receive a copy. Exported or shared copies remain with the destination until you delete them there.

Files you open, labels and the widget

When you open or share a G-code or 3MF file with SpoolNest, the app reads it on your device to find the filament weight, print time, material and slicer name. It keeps only those values with the print you log. The file itself is not copied or uploaded.

Spool labels are made on your device. The QR code contains only a link to that spool's identifier in SpoolNest on your phone; it does not contain your other records. To share or print a label, the app saves the image temporarily in its private cache and hands it to the app or printing service you choose.

The home-screen widget shows spool names and the amount left. Anyone who can see your home screen can see it; remove the widget if you don't want that.

Advertising and privacy choices

The app uses Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). A banner can appear separately from the main task content and navigation. There are no interstitial, rewarded or app-open ads. When SpoolNest Pro has been confirmed, the app does not request banners. Core features remain available without internet access and if ad or consent requests fail.

Google's advertising SDK can collect and share IP address (which may indicate approximate location), device and applicable account identifiers such as advertising ID and app set ID, app/ad interactions, and diagnostics such as crashes and performance. Google uses SDK data for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention, and encrypts its transmission using TLS. We do not pass your saved recipes, notes or inventory to the SDK.

Where required, UMP presents the privacy message configured for this app before advertisements are requested. Revisit Privacy choices in Settings when Google requires that entry point. Declining optional advertising purposes does not lock the app's tools. Some limited ads may still be permitted depending on your choices, location and Google's settings. You can also manage your advertising ID in Android settings.

Google and any advertising providers disclosed in the consent message determine retention and privacy handling for data they receive. Use Google's privacy controls for that data. See Google's privacy policy and Google's advertising information.

Optional SpoolNest Pro purchase

SpoolNest Pro is an optional one-time purchase or yearly subscription. It removes banner ads and unlocks G-code and 3MF logging, spool labels, the home-screen widget and more than one printer. Spool tracking, weighing, print logging, costs, stats and backups stay free with ads. Earlier Remove Ads purchases include SpoolNest Pro. Google Play shows the local price and renewal terms before purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled; use Settings → SpoolNest Pro, or Google Play's subscription settings, to manage them. Each app has its own purchases.

Google Play processes payments under its own terms and privacy policy. The app does not receive your card number or payment-account password. It queries Google Play for this app's purchase and subscription status and acknowledges completed purchases. Signed receipt data, purchase tokens and the last confirmation time are stored privately on the device to check access to SpoolNest Pro and support a short offline confirmation window. This information is not included in backups, sent to a YTHQ Studios server, or passed to AdMob. Reinstalling or changing phones may require Restore purchase using the Google Play account that made the purchase.

Google keeps payment and transaction information according to its own policies. As the publisher, YTHQ Studios may have access to order and subscription information in Google's developer tools for billing, support and fraud prevention. Clearing app storage removes local receipt data; it does not cancel a subscription or delete Google's transaction records.

Contact and changes

For questions, contact support@ythqstudios.com. We do not hold a server copy of the records described above. Remove them using the app or Android settings, and remove any exported copies at their storage destination. We will update this page and its effective date if the app's features or data practices change.

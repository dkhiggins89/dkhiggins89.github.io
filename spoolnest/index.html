SpoolNest

Filament Tracker for Android

SpoolNest tracks every spool, logs each print and works out what it cost, so you never start a long print on a spool that's about to run out.

Get it on Google PlayFree to download

New in the latest update

  • Weigh a spool on a kitchen scale
  • New Stats tab with 30-day totals and use by material
  • Stock forecast at your printing pace
  • Shareable “running low” shopping list

What it does

Spool inventory

Every spool with material, brand, colour, price and grams remaining at a glance.

Weigh on a scale

Pop a spool on a kitchen scale and SpoolNest subtracts the empty spool for you.

Print log and costs

Log each print and see the cost of filament and electricity.

Stats

Prints, grams used, spend, use by material and how long your stock will last.

Running-low alerts

A warning when a spool drops under 100 g, plus a shareable shopping list.

Print cost calculator

Price a print before you start and check you have enough on the spool.

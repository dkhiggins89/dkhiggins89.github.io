Night Flight — Privacy policy

App: Airplane Cabin Sounds: Sleep (Night Flight)

Package: com.ythqstudios.airplanecabinsounds

Publisher: YTHQ Studios

Support and privacy contact: fitandemapp@gmail.com

Effective date: 14 October 2026

1. Listening and information stored on your device

Night Flight creates airplane cabin ambience on your Android device. You do not need an account or internet connection to listen. The app stores your mixer levels, preset and chosen timer duration locally. If you import a personal call bell, the app reads the file you select through Android's document picker, decodes it and stores a private audio copy on your device. We do not upload that clip, its filename or your mixer preferences. We do not operate a server that receives your sound mix or listening history.

The original sounds are generated locally. The app does not record your microphone, use speech synthesis, or access contacts or precise location. Playback state and an active timer are not restored after process termination. Audio never starts automatically after a restart.

Local preferences remain until you clear app data or uninstall. Restore original chime deletes the imported audio copy; clear app data or uninstall removes preferences and the saved clip. Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup are excluded by the app's backup configuration.

2. Advertising and consent services

The app integrates Google Mobile Ads Next-Gen SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). These services can use internet access independently of local listening. Banner ads can appear on the visible mixer, including during audio playback. There are no interstitial, rewarded or app-open ads. Banners are removed in Settings, in dim mode, while the app is backgrounded or locked, and when a saved lifetime ad-removal purchase is active. No advertisements are displayed by the media notification or on the lock screen.

From version 1.1.0, optional lifetime ad removal uses Google Play Billing. Google Play processes payment under its own terms and privacy policy; we do not receive your card details. The app receives signed purchase information, product identifiers, status and purchase tokens from Google Play to verify and acknowledge purchases and restore access. Purchase-related information is processed by Google for checkout, acknowledgement, restoration and fraud prevention. We do not send receipts to a publisher-operated server, create an app account or log purchase tokens. Only an ad-removal entitlement flag is saved in private device preferences; full receipts are not stored by this app. Google retains its own transaction records. Buying and restoring need Google Play connectivity and the purchasing Google account. Saved ad removal works offline; an online ownership check can update access after cancellation or refund. Clearing app data or reinstalling removes the local flag, but a valid purchase can be restored through Google Play.

Google's advertising SDK can collect and share:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate approximate location;
  • device and applicable account identifiers, including advertising ID and app set ID;
  • app and ad interaction information, such as launches and ad taps;
  • diagnostic information about the app and SDK, such as performance, errors, launch time and resource use.

Google uses this information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. UMP contacts Google to determine applicable privacy requirements, present messages and manage consent choices. Consent choices and related identifiers may be stored on the device and processed by Google. Google and the advertising technology providers identified in the consent message may receive advertising-related information. We do not add a separate analytics SDK or additional mediation adapters.

Google documents encryption of advertising SDK data in transit using TLS. The app disallows cleartext network traffic. Please also read Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps.

3. Your ad privacy choices and purposes

Where required, including in the UK, EEA and Switzerland, UMP presents choices before advertisements are requested. The app requests ads only when UMP permits them. You can revisit applicable choices through Settings → Ad privacy options and withdraw consent there. Android settings let you reset or delete the advertising ID.

Refusing optional advertising purposes does not limit the mixer, bells, timers or offline listening. Depending on your region and choices, Google may still permit limited ads. Consent services may contact Google even if an advertisement is not shown, so refusing personalisation does not mean every network request or every category of SDK data processing stops.

Consent is used for advertising personalisation and device storage where required. Privacy-choice processing supports compliance with applicable obligations. Google may also process data on other applicable legal bases, including legitimate interests such as service security and fraud prevention, as explained in its policy and the consent message. These interests concern maintaining a secure service and detecting invalid or abusive advertising activity; they do not give us access to your private audio files. Local mixer settings and the file you deliberately select are used to provide the functions you request.

4. Permissions

Internet and network-state permissions support advertising and consent. A media-playback foreground service and a partial wake lock support background listening and reliable sleep timing with the screen locked. Notifications provide playback controls. The document picker grants access to a file you choose without broad access to your media library. No microphone, contacts or precise location permission is requested.

5. Retention and international processing

We do not hold a cloud account database or uploaded audio. Google retains advertising and consent information according to its own policies, legal obligations and service needs. We do not control Google's retention periods and cannot promise deletion of information held by Google. Google may process information outside your country. Its privacy policy explains relevant international transfer safeguards and how to obtain further information about them.

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you provide. We use it to answer support enquiries and handle privacy requests, relying on legitimate interests in providing support and on legal obligations where applicable. Email is processed through our email provider, Google/Gmail. We keep correspondence for as long as needed to resolve the enquiry and meet applicable legal requirements; we do not ask you to send your personal audio file unless it is necessary and you choose to do so.

6. Rights and requests

Depending on your location and the processing involved, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to processing, receive portable data, and withdraw consent. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests. Contact fitandemapp@gmail.com for support or to make a privacy request. We may need enough information to verify and handle your request; we do not need your password or payment details. Some rights are subject to legal limits.

There is no Night Flight user account to delete. Use the local deletion controls described above. You can contact Google regarding information it controls and use the controls in its privacy policy and Android settings. We can explain the app's integration and respond to requests concerning information we hold, but cannot directly erase all data held independently by Google.

You may complain to your local data protection authority. In the UK, you can contact the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). In the EEA or Switzerland, you can contact the relevant supervisory authority for your location.

7. Audience and automated decisions

Night Flight is intended for adults seeking sleep ambience and is not directed to children. It is not a medical treatment or a guarantee of sleep. We do not use the app to make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on you. Google's advertising systems may personalise advertising according to the choices you make and their policies.

This page itself contains no advertising, tracking scripts or publisher-set cookies. Hosting this page does not upload your app audio or mixer preferences.

We will update this policy when the app's processing changes. The effective date at the top identifies the current version. If a material change requires new notice or consent, we will provide it through the appropriate app or consent flow.

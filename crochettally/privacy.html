Projects, patterns and local storage

CrochetTally stores project names, notes, yarn and hook details, photos, attached PDF or image patterns, counts, reminders, reading positions and timer totals in private storage on your device. It does not require an app account or operate a project server. We do not upload this project content. The app does not access your contacts, microphone or precise location. You choose each photo, pattern and backup through Android's file picker; the app does not request broad access to your files.

Android cloud backup and automatic device transfer are disabled for this app. Export a backup before moving devices or uninstalling. Exported backups include your project content, including photos and patterns. You choose the destination, which may be a storage provider with its own privacy policy. Copies you export remain where you save them until you delete them. Imports read only the backup you select, and can replace matching local projects after confirmation.

Deleting a project removes its saved data and attached assets from the app. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes its local projects and settings. These actions do not delete backups you exported elsewhere. A running project timer continues until you pause it; this is stored locally and is not sent to the publisher.

Advertising and consent

CrochetTally includes Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform. Banners can appear on the project library and project-details screens. There are no interstitial, rewarded or app-open ads. No banners appear on the counter or pattern-reader screens, or for users with an active ad-removal entitlement. The app does not request banners while it is backgrounded. Project features remain available when you decline optional advertising consent or have no network connection.

Google's advertising SDK can collect and share IP addresses (which may indicate approximate location), app and ad interactions, diagnostics such as crashes and performance, and device or applicable account identifiers such as advertising ID and app set ID. Google processes this information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS. We do not add a separate analytics SDK or send project content to advertising services.

Where required, the app presents Google's consent message and offers consent, refusal and management choices. You can revisit Ad privacy choices in Settings when Google requires that entry point. Some limited advertising may remain permitted after optional purposes are refused. Your Android settings also let you reset or delete the advertising ID. Google and its disclosed advertising providers control their own data retention and privacy processes.

Google advertising information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Optional one-time ad removal

Google Play processes the optional permanent ad-removal purchase. It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. The current local price is supplied by Google Play and confirmed in checkout. We do not receive your payment-card details.

The app receives product identifiers, signed purchase data, purchase status and tokens from Google Play to verify ownership, acknowledge purchases and restore ad-free access. Purchase-related information is processed by Google for payment, app functionality, security and fraud prevention. The app does not send receipts to a publisher-operated server or log purchase tokens. Only an ad-free entitlement flag is stored in private device preferences; full receipts are not saved by the app.

Purchases and restoration require Google Play connectivity and the purchasing Google account. A previously confirmed entitlement works offline. A later successful online ownership query can update access after a refund or revocation. Clearing app data or reinstalling removes the local flag, but a valid purchase can be restored through Google Play. Google retains its own transaction records under its policies. Removing ads stops banners; Google Play may still process purchase and restoration requests.

Contact and changes

For questions about this app or policy, contact fitandemapp@gmail.com. The publisher does not hold a server copy of your crochet projects. Remove local data in the app or Android settings, and delete exported copies from their storage destinations as needed. For advertising and transaction data handled by Google, use Google's privacy and account controls.

We may update this policy when the app's features or data practices change. The current policy is available online and an offline copy is included in the app.

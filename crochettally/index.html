CrochetTallyRow Counter · Android

A crochet row counter that keeps up with your hook.

Count rows, rounds and stitches, read your pattern on the same screen and never lose your place again. CrochetTally works offline and keeps every project on your phone.

What you can do

Big, tappable counters

Rows, rounds and stitches with a huge +1 button, undo and quick editing.

Count hands-free

Volume up adds a row and volume down takes one away, so your hands stay on the hook.

Patterns beside your count

Open a PDF or photo of your pattern with a line marker that remembers where you were.

Repeats and reminders

Named counters for repeats, and reminders such as “increase every 6 rows”.

Stitch and hook reference

UK and US terms side by side, plus a metric, US and UK hook size chart.

Pace and project timer

Time each project and see rows per hour and roughly how long until you finish.

Price

Free to download with ads. CrochetTally Pro is a one-off purchase that removes ads and adds the home-screen counter widget plus unlimited reminders and named counters.

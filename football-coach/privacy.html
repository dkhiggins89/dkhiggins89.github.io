Football Coach: privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy explains what the Football Coach app for Android (package com.aistudio.footballcoachmatchtools.vgtxda) does with information. The app is made by YouthTeamHQ Limited, trading as YTHQ Studios ("we", "us"). We are the data controller for the limited processing described here.

Summary

  • You don't need an account, and there is no sign-in.
  • Your squad, line-ups, minutes, goals and assists are stored on your phone. We don't run a server, and we can't see your records.
  • The app shows Google AdMob banner ads unless you've bought ad removal. Google asks for your ad consent where the law requires it.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

Information you enter

The app stores the following in a private database on your device:

  • player names, squad numbers and preferred positions
  • matches: opponent name, date, formation and match length
  • line-ups, substitutes, unavailable players and the seconds each player was on the pitch
  • goals, assists and substitutions, with the minute they happened

This information isn't sent to us. It only leaves the app if you choose to share a report, for example in a messaging app. Whatever you share is then handled by the app you share it with.

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include the app's data in your device backup, and it may be copied to a new phone when you transfer your data. Google handles these backups under its own terms, and you can turn them off in your phone's settings.

Players who are children

The app is meant for adult coaches and team managers. It isn't designed for children to use. If you enter the names of players who are under 18, make sure you do so in line with your club's rules and with any permission needed from parents or guardians. Use first names or initials if you prefer. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Unless you've removed ads, the app shows banner ads from Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, your IP address (which gives an approximate location), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Read how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Ad consent (Google User Messaging Platform)

If you're in the UK, the EEA, Switzerland or another region where consent is needed, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask whether you agree to personalised ads. It stores your choice on the device. Where it applies, you can change your choice from the app's privacy settings option. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings, under Privacy, then Ads.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

You can remove ads and unlock the season playing-time report with a one-off purchase or a yearly subscription. Google Play processes the payment. The app only checks with Google Play whether you own the purchase, and remembers the result on your device. You can manage or cancel a subscription in Google Play.

Reviews (Google Play In-App Review)

After you've used the app for a while, it may ask you to rate it using Google Play's in-app review screen. The app stores a note on the device so it doesn't ask too often. Google Play handles any rating or review you leave.

Legal bases (UK and EEA)

  • Contract: to provide the app and any purchase you make.
  • Consent: for personalised advertising, where the law requires it.
  • Legitimate interests: to show non-personalised ads that fund the free version, and to keep purchases secure.

Keeping and deleting your information

You can delete players and matches in the app at any time. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on your device, apart from any copy in your own Android backup. Google keeps advertising and purchase records under its own privacy policy.

Your rights

Under UK and EU data protection law you can ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data, and you can object to some processing. Because your records stay on your device, we don't hold any of your squad or match data. For questions about advertising or purchase data, contact us and we'll point you to the right place at Google. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

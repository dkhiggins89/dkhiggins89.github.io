Oneira — Privacy policy

App: Oneira: Learn to Lucid Dream

Package: com.ythqstudios.oneira

Publisher: YouthTeamHQ Limited, trading as YTHQ Studios

Support and privacy contact: fitandemapp@gmail.com

Effective date: 12 November 2026

1. Your dream journal and settings

Oneira helps you practise lucid dreaming with guides, a dream journal, reality-check reminders, visualisations and sleep sounds. You do not need an account. Dream entries (text, moods, dream signs, ratings and the technique you used) and your app settings are stored in the app's private storage on your device. We do not operate a server that receives your journal, your settings or your listening history, and we never see what you write.

Android may include the journal and settings in your device's own backup (Google's Android backup service and device-to-device transfer) if backup is turned on in your phone's settings. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account and its privacy policy; we cannot access it. You can turn Android backup off in your phone's settings.

The journal leaves the app only if you choose to export it, in which case Android's share sheet sends the text to the app you pick. Settings → Delete all dreams permanently erases all entries from the device. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes the journal and settings from the device.

2. Sounds, narration and dictation

All sleep sounds and music are generated on your device in real time; no audio is streamed. Spoken guidance uses your phone's built-in text-to-speech engine. If you tap the microphone in the journal, Oneira asks your phone's speech-recognition app (usually Google) to turn your speech into text. Oneira itself does not record or store audio and does not request microphone permission; the speech-recognition app processes your voice under its own privacy policy and may do so online. Only the resulting text is returned to Oneira and saved if you keep it.

3. Reminders and notifications

If you turn on reality-check reminders, Oneira schedules notifications on your device at the times you choose. A notification is also shown while sleep sounds or the night routine are playing, so they can continue with the screen off. Notification settings are stored locally.

4. Advertising and consent

The free version shows ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) and asks for consent through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) where required. Banner ads appear on the main tabs. Occasional full-screen ads may appear after you finish an action such as saving a dream or completing a practice. Ads are never shown during bedtime routines, sleep sounds or guided night audio.

Google's advertising SDK can collect and share:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate approximate location;
  • device identifiers, including the advertising ID and app set ID;
  • app and ad interaction information, such as app launches and ad taps;
  • diagnostic information about the app and SDK, such as performance and crashes.

Google uses this information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Your journal content is never shared with the advertising SDK. We do not add a separate analytics SDK or ad mediation partners. Please read Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps.

5. Your ad privacy choices

Where required, including in the UK, EEA and Switzerland, a consent message appears before ads are requested, and ads are requested only when it permits them. You can change your choices at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. Refusing personalised ads does not limit any feature of the app; Google may still show non-personalised or limited ads.

6. Ad-free subscription

An optional yearly subscription removes ads. Google Play handles payment under its own terms and privacy policy; we never receive your card details. The app receives purchase status, product identifiers and purchase tokens from Google Play to confirm, acknowledge and restore the subscription. We do not send these to a server of our own. Only an ad-free flag is saved on the device. You can manage or cancel the subscription in the Google Play Store.

7. Permissions

Internet and network-state access support ads, consent and purchases. Notifications show reminders and playback controls. The foreground media-playback service and wake lock let sounds and the night routine continue with the screen off. Oneira does not request microphone, contacts, location, camera or storage permissions.

8. Retention and international processing

We do not hold any account database or copies of your journal. Data stored on your device stays until you delete it, clear app data or uninstall. Google keeps advertising, consent, purchase and backup information according to its own policies and may process it outside your country; its privacy policy explains the safeguards used.

If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write. We use it only to answer you and to handle privacy requests, and we keep the correspondence only as long as needed. Email is handled through our provider, Google (Gmail).

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests. Contact fitandemapp@gmail.com for support or to make a request. We will never ask for your password or payment details. Because your journal stays on your device, you control it directly through the app's export and delete options. For information held by Google, use the controls in your Google account and Android settings.

You can complain to your data protection authority. In the UK this is the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

10. Audience and health

Oneira is intended for adults and is not directed at children. It offers general wellbeing and sleep-practice guidance and is not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. We do not use the app to make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on you.

11. This page and changes

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process request information such as IP addresses to run and secure its service (see the GitHub privacy statement). The page contains no ads, tracking scripts or cookies set by us. We will update this policy if the app's data handling changes; the effective date above shows the current version.

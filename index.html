About the studio

YTHQ started as YouthTeamHQ, making an attendance register and a playing-time tracker for grassroots football coaches. The same approach now covers other hobbies: crochet, sewing, 3D printing, pizza, coffee, allotments and sleep.

YTHQ Studios is an independent developer based in the UK. If something in one of the apps doesn't work the way you expect, email us and we'll look into it.

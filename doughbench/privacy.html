Your records stay on your device

The app stores dough formulas, recipe names and notes, planned preparation durations, start times and checklist progress in private device storage. It does not require an account or send these records to a YTHQ Studios server. There is no separate analytics SDK, cloud sync, microphone, camera, contacts or precise-location feature.

Automatic Android cloud backup and device transfer are disabled. Export a backup before changing devices or uninstalling. Deleting an item removes it from the app; clearing app storage or uninstalling removes all local records and preferences. These actions do not delete backups you exported elsewhere.

Backup files and sharing

You choose each backup file and its destination through Android's document picker. An export contains the app's saved records and their local identifiers. It does not contain active timer state or screen preferences. The chosen storage provider may have its own privacy policy. Restoring a compatible backup replaces the app's saved records only after your confirmation. The app does not request broad file-storage access.

Where a Share action is available, you choose which app or person receives the displayed recipe text using Android's share sheet. We do not receive a copy. Exported or shared copies remain with the destination until you delete them there.

Advertising and privacy choices

The app uses Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). A banner can appear separately from the main task content and navigation. There are no interstitial, rewarded or app-open ads. An optional yearly Remove Ads subscription is available from version 1.2.0; when ad-free access has been confirmed, the app does not request banners. Core features remain available without internet access and if ad or consent requests fail.

Google's advertising SDK can collect and share IP address (which may indicate approximate location), device and applicable account identifiers such as advertising ID and app set ID, app/ad interactions, and diagnostics such as crashes and performance. Google uses SDK data for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention, and encrypts its transmission using TLS. We do not pass your saved recipes, notes or inventory to the SDK.

Where required, UMP presents the privacy message configured for this app before advertisements are requested. Revisit Privacy choices in Settings when Google requires that entry point. Declining optional advertising purposes does not lock the app's tools. Some limited ads may still be permitted depending on your choices, location and Google's settings. You can also manage your advertising ID in Android settings.

Google and any advertising providers disclosed in the consent message determine retention and privacy handling for data they receive. Use Google's privacy controls for that data. See Google's privacy policy and Google's advertising information.

Optional Remove Ads subscription

An optional yearly subscription removes banner ads in this app while the subscription is active. All tools remain available free with ads. Google Play shows the local price and renewal terms before purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled; use Settings → Remove ads → Manage or cancel subscription, or Google Play's subscription settings, to manage them. Each app has its own subscription.

Google Play processes payments under its own terms and privacy policy. The app does not receive your card number or payment-account password. It queries Google Play for this app's purchase and subscription status and acknowledges completed purchases. Signed receipt data, purchase tokens and the last confirmation time are stored privately on the device to check ad-free access and support a short offline confirmation window. This information is not included in notebook exports, sent to a YTHQ Studios server, or passed to AdMob. Reinstalling or changing phones may require Restore purchase using the Google Play account that purchased the subscription.

Google keeps payment and transaction information according to its own policies. As the publisher, YTHQ Studios may have access to order and subscription information in Google's developer tools for billing, support and fraud prevention. Clearing app storage removes local receipt data; it does not cancel a subscription or delete Google's transaction records.

Contact and changes

For questions, contact fitandemapp@gmail.com. We do not hold a server copy of the records described above. Remove them using the app or Android settings, and remove any exported copies at their storage destination. We will update this page and its effective date if the app's features or data practices change.
